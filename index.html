    04 / TECHNICAL REFERENCE

    Methodology & scope

    Deterministic rules and explicit exclusion criteria. This tool intentionally separates measurement, screening, interpretation and final regulatory decisions.

    ICH Q1A(R2) / §2.1.7.1 & §2.2.7.1

    Storage conditions

    For eligible general room-temperature products, select long-term 25°C/60% RH or 30°C/65% RH, plus accelerated 40°C/75% RH. Intermediate 30°C/65% RH applies conditionally only when long-term is 25°C/60% RH.

    V0.1 METHOD / DESCRIPTIVE

    Measured change

    Absolute change = result − baseline. For drug-product assay screening, relative magnitude (%) = 100 × |result − initial| / |initial|, for a documented comparable assay measurement basis. A nonzero condition-specific time-zero measurement is required.

    ICH Q1A(R2) / §2.1.7.1

    Drug substance

    Significant change at accelerated conditions is defined as failure to meet its specification. This tool requires user-entered acceptance criteria. It does not apply the drug-product assay threshold to substances.

    ICH Q1A(R2) / §2.2.7.1

    Drug product

    Potential significant-change screens cover assay, degradation products and context-relevant acceptance criteria for physical/functionality, pH and dissolution. Dissolution requires the appropriate 12-unit test; a single result alone does not verify that method requirement.

    ICH Q1E / FUTURE VERSION

    No automatic shelf-life conversion

    Time-temperature equivalence, Arrhenius kinetics and unqualified Q10 factors are not used. Regression and confidence-bound shelf-life evaluation are out of scope until separately verified and appropriately qualified.

    APPLICABILITY GATE

    Unsupported cases

    Refrigerated, frozen, aqueous products in semipermeable containers, biologics and complex modalities require specialized study designs. V0.1 does not render these categories eligible by substituting the general room-temperature conditions.
